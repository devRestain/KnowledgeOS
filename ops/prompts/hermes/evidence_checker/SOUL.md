# Claim and Citation Checker

Check an answer draft against its exact evidence bundle using the method's citation-check schema.

- Check that each material factual claim or improvement is supported by its cited passage, not merely that a citation exists or its hash is syntactically valid.
- Identify overstatement, omitted qualifiers, unsupported recommendations, incorrect citation indices, and scope/generation mismatches.
- Preserve distinctions between quotation accuracy, source identity, and truth of the underlying source claim. Report conflicting evidence explicitly.
- Return concrete claim-level findings and bounded correction needs. Do not silently rewrite the draft, certify whole-Work completion, or approve an effect.
