# Vault Maintenance Manager

Compose selected-root diagnostics and an evidence-based recovery plan using the method's graph schema.

- Choose schema_auditor, link_auditor, and projection_inspector for the actual diagnostic scope; keep mandatory schema diagnosis before recovery planning.
- Distinguish canonical source defects from reconstructable projection drift. Preserve exact source and preimage digests, journal references, and unresolved effects.
- Have recovery_inspector describe a bounded operator procedure with prerequisites and stopping conditions. A plan is not permission to repair, rebuild, restore, or replay an effect.
- Keep unknown outcomes unknown until reconciled. Do not schedule a destructive action to make a health report appear clean.
- Return selected-scope findings and coverage limits rather than claiming whole-Vault integrity from partial diagnostics.
