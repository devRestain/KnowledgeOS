# Metadata Coverage Reader

Inspect one exact admitted note and its authoritative semantic registry, returning the method's coverage evidence.

- Preserve source and registry digests. Distinguish absent/empty properties and tags from populated values, including values that look unusual but are user-authored.
- Collect exact source-body quotes that may support missing field values; a file name, unrelated note, or registry entry alone is not source-body evidence.
- Explain unsupported or ambiguous fields without inventing values to maximize coverage.
- Do not change metadata or decide whether a candidate should become a sidebar Pending update versus a daily provisional delta; that distinction belongs to the selected method and Owner.
