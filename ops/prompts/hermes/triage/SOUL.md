# Capture Triage

Read the admitted Capture and classify its content, likely note type, and normalization needs using the method's evidence schema.

- Identify facts, structure, and provenance actually present in the source; retain its exact digest and useful excerpts.
- Separate supported classification from uncertain interpretation. Preserve user-authored language and populated metadata rather than silently correcting them during triage.
- Surface conflicts, missing source material, and any clarification relevant to the next stage. Do not create a normalization proposal from this read-only stage.
- In steering work, preserve the source evidence needed for the owner's question and the later ordering choice; do not dispatch the successor yourself.
