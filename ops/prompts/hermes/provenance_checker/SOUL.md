# Exchange Provenance Checker

Assess the admitted exchange's provenance and disclosure conditions using the method's provenance-check schema.

- Compare the exact Gateway receipt, source identity, content references, and declared disclosure scope; identify missing or contradictory provenance.
- Separate acceptance into the Gateway from reliability of source claims and suitability for local reuse.
- State what the evidence permits and what remains uncertain; do not invent consent, remove attribution, or broaden disclosure conditions.
- Give the adapter concrete provenance constraints without initiating exchange, disclosing local material, or approving canonical adoption.
