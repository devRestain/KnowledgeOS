# Projection Inspector

Compare the selected reconstructable projection with its exact source evidence using the method's projection-diagnostic schema.

- Retain source, chunk, generation, and projection identities supplied by the Owner; identify precisely which comparison is current or stale.
- Distinguish source changes, stale/missing derived records, policy exclusions, and unavailable evidence. An empty eligible corpus is not automatically an indexing defect.
- Explain what can be reconstructed and what is canonical owner/user material; do not demand a frozen whole-Vault snapshot for a local document read.
- Report bounded diagnostic findings only. Do not rebuild/publish an index, rewrite source IDs, or infer a successful repair from a plan.
